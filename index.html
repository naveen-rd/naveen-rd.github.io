03 · Personal project

Cogdex, on my own time.

Open source. A Mac app I built because I was tired of hunting through PDFs for a date.

Open source · Mac app

A local assistant for the papers in the drawer.

Cogdex indexes passports, insurance cards, medical and tax files on your Mac. You ask in plain English. It answers from those files, with the source named. Semantic RAG over documents, plus facts you tell it once. Llama and the embeddings stay on the machine. Nothing is uploaded.

I built the Python backend, the search index in SQLite, Apple Vision OCR for scans, and the macOS app. It is a nights-and-weekends project, in private beta.
